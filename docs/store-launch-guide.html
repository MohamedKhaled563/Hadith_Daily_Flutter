A

Create the two developer accounts

You need one account with Google and one with Apple. They are the "shop licences" that let you sell in each store. Start both on day one, because each takes a few days to be approved.

First decision: personal or company account?

Both stores ask this. It decides whose name appears under the app in the store.

Pick this if Prodkt Studio is a registered company

Organization

  • Store shows "Prodkt Studio"
  • Google skips the 14-day, 12-tester test
  • Needs a free D-U-N-S number (a company ID). Getting one can take 1–4 weeks
  • Needs company papers and a company website
Pick this if there is no registered company

Personal (Individual)

  • Store shows your own full legal name
  • Approved in 1–2 days
  • Google requires 12 testers for 14 days before going live (step B8)
  • Only your national ID or passport is needed

Organization only: request the D-U-N-S number first, free, through Apple's D-U-N-S lookup. The same number works for Google.

Google Play developer account

  1. Prepare a Google account just for publishing

    You

    Why: this account will own the app forever. Use a company Gmail such as prodktstudio@gmail.com, not a personal one.

    • Turn on 2-step verification in that Google account.
    • Have ready: national ID or passport, a phone number, a card, and an Android phone.

  2. Sign up and pay $25

    You$25

    Why: this is the one-time registration fee that opens your Play Console.

    • Open play.google.com/console/signup and sign in.
    • Choose Yourself (personal) or An organization or business.
    • Fill in the developer name. This is shown publicly in the store.
    • Fill in a contact email and phone. Google verifies both with codes.
    • Pay the $25 by card.

  3. Verify your identity and your Android phone

    You

    Why: Google will not let you publish until it confirms you are a real person with a real phone.

    • Upload a photo of your ID when asked. The name must match the card holder for personal accounts.
    • Install the Google Play Console app on your Android phone and sign in with the same account. This verifies the device.
    • Wait for the approval email. It usually takes 1–2 working days.

Apple Developer Program

  1. Prepare an Apple Account with two-factor sign-in

    You

    Why: Apple requires two-factor authentication on the account that owns the app.

    • Use an existing Apple Account or create one at account.apple.com.
    • Turn on two-factor authentication (needs an iPhone or a phone number).
    • Your account name must be your real legal name. Nicknames get the enrollment rejected.

  2. Enroll and pay $99

    You$99 / year

    Why: only paid members can upload apps to the App Store.

    • Easiest, if you have an iPhone: install the Apple Developer app → Account → Enroll Now. It scans your ID and you pay inside the app.
    • Without an iPhone: go to developer.apple.com/programs/enroll → Start your enrollment.
    • Choose Individual or Organization (same choice as above).
    • Pay the $99. Approval: often same day for individuals, up to 2 weeks for organizations.

B

Release on Google Play (Android)

Start this once Google approves your account. Most of it is filling forms in Play Console. The developer has already written the exact answers in docs/play-console-answers.md, so you can copy them.

  1. Create the app's signing key

    DeveloperYou keep the passwords

    Why: every update must be signed with the same key. It is the app's permanent "signature".

    • The developer runs one command on the computer. It creates a small file named tayeb-qalbak-upload.jks.
    • You choose two passwords and save them in a password manager.
    • Back up the file in two safe places (for example Google Drive and a USB stick).
    For the developer: commands 
    keytool -genkey -v -keystore "%USERPROFILE%\tayeb-qalbak-upload.jks" -storetype JKS -keyalg RSA -keysize 2048 -validity 10000 -alias upload

    Then create android/key.properties (it is git-ignored):

    storePassword=…
keyPassword=…
keyAlias=upload
storeFile=C:/Users/Mohamed Khaled/tayeb-qalbak-upload.jks

    Full runbook: docs/android-release-reminding-steps.md, steps 1–2.

  2. Build the upload file (.aab)

    Developer

    Why: Google Play accepts one file type, the "app bundle". This is what you upload.

    For the developer: commands 
    flutter build appbundle --release
keytool -printcert -jarfile build/app/outputs/bundle/release/app-release.aab

    The owner printed must be you, not CN=Android Debug. The build refuses to run without key.properties, on purpose. Also run flutter build apk --release then pwsh tool/verify_release_apk.ps1.

  3. Create the app in Play Console

    You

    Why: this creates the empty store page that everything else fills in.

    • Play Console → Create app.
    • App name طيّب قلبك, default language Arabic, App, Free.
    • Tick the two declarations and press Create app.

  5. Fill the store page: text and pictures

    YouDeveloper helps with images

    Why: this is what people see before they install.

    • Grow users → Store presence → Main store listing.
    • Short and full description: ready to paste in docs/play-console-answers.md.
    • App icon 512 × 512 PNG.
    • Feature graphic 1024 × 500 (a banner shown at the top of the listing).
    • 2 to 8 phone screenshots of the real app. Good choices: home, a daily message, the forty hadiths list, community, reminders.

  6. Upload to "Internal testing" and try it yourself

    You

    Why: a private test that needs no Google review. You install the real store version on your own phone.

    • Test and release → Testing → Internal testing → Create new release.
    • Accept Play App Signing if asked. Google then keeps a backup of the key, so a lost key can be replaced.
    • Upload the .aab from step 2, then add your own email in the Testers tab.
    • Open the invite link on your phone and install.
    • Check: reminders arrive with the phone locked, Google sign-in works, posting and deleting an account work.

  7. Connect Google's signing key to Firebase

    DeveloperNeeds Play account

    Why: Google re-signs the app for the store. Without this step, "Sign in with Google" fails for every store user.

    • Play Console → Test and release → App integrity → App signing → copy the SHA-1 and SHA-256.
    • Paste both into Firebase → Project settings → Android app → Add fingerprint.
    • Download the new google-services.json into android/app/ and rebuild.

  8. Closed test: 12 testers for 14 days

    YouPersonal accounts only

    Why: Google requires new personal accounts to prove real people used the app before it goes public. Organization accounts skip this step.

    • Testing → Closed testing → create a track, upload the same .aab.
    • Add at least 12 Gmail addresses of friends or family who own Android phones.
    • Each one opens the invite link, joins, installs, and keeps it installed for 14 days in a row.
    • Ask them to open it a few times. Google looks for real use.

    Google's rule: App testing requirements for new personal accounts.

    Collect the 12 emails now, while waiting for account approval. This is the slowest part of the Android launch.

  9. Apply for production, then publish

    You

    Why: this sends the app to Google's review and then to the public store.

    • Personal accounts: Dashboard → Apply for production, answer a few questions about the test (1–7 days).
    • Then Test and release → Production → Create new release → add the same build.
    • Set rollout to 20% first. Raise it to 100% after a few days without crashes.
    • Send for review. First review usually takes 1–7 days. You get an email when it is live.

Release on the App Store (iPhone)

iPhone apps can only be built on a Mac. We work on Windows, so a cloud service called Codemagic rents us a Mac for each build. The build file for it is now in the project (codemagic.yaml).

  1. Register the app's ID with Apple

    DeveloperNeeds Apple account

    Why: Apple must know the app's unique name and which features it uses, such as "Sign in with Apple".

    • Certificates, Identifiers & Profiles → Identifiers → + → App IDs → App.
    • Bundle ID (explicit): com.prodktstudio.tayebqalbak
    • Tick Sign in with Apple only. No push notifications needed.

  2. Create the app in App Store Connect

    You

    Why: this is the App Store version of the store page. It also gives the app a number the developer needs.

    • App Store Connect → Apps → + → New App.
    • Platform iOS, name طيّب قلبك, primary language Arabic, bundle ID from step 1, SKU tayebqalbak, full access.
    • Open App Information and copy the Apple ID number (about 10 digits). Send it to the developer.

    Simplified sketch. Apple's guide: Add a new app.

  3. Put the App Store link in the app

    DeveloperNeeds the number from step 2

    Why: when someone shares a message from an iPhone, the shared text includes the download link.

    For the developer: the change

    In lib/core/app_links.dart:

    static const String? appStore = 'https://apps.apple.com/app/id<APPLE_ID>';

  4. Turn on Apple sign-in in Firebase

    Developer

    Why: Apple requires "Sign in with Apple" because the app offers Google sign-in. The code is ready; Firebase must accept it.

    • Firebase → Authentication → Sign-in method → Add Apple → Enable → Save.

  5. Create an API key so Codemagic can upload for you

    YouDeveloper

    Why: the key lets Codemagic sign the app and send it to Apple without a Mac.

    • App Store Connect → Users and Access → Integrations → App Store Connect API → Generate key, access App Manager.
    • Download the .p8 file. Apple lets you download it only once. Store it safely.
    • Note the Key ID and Issuer ID shown on the same page.

  6. Build the iPhone app in Codemagic

    DeveloperFree tier

    Why: this turns the code into the iPhone app file and sends it to Apple's TestFlight.

    • Sign in at codemagic.io with GitHub and add the Hadith_Daily_Flutter repository.
    • Team settings → Integrations → Developer Portal → add the key from step 5. Name it exactly Tayeb Qalbak ASC key.
    • In the app's settings, switch to codemagic.yaml. Start the workflow iOS release (TestFlight).
    • About 20–30 minutes later the build appears in App Store Connect → TestFlight.

    Codemagic's guide: iOS code signing.

  7. Test on a real iPhone with TestFlight

    You

    Why: the same check as Android's internal test, before Apple reviews it.

    • App Store Connect → TestFlight → Internal Testing → add your Apple Account email.
    • Install the TestFlight app on an iPhone and install طيّب قلبك from it.
    • Check reminders, Google and Apple sign-in, sharing a card, deleting an account.
    • Take the store screenshots on this iPhone (a large one such as a Pro Max is best).

  8. Fill the App Store page and privacy answers

    You

    Why: Apple shows these on the store page and checks them during review.

    Where in App Store ConnectWhat to enter
    App InformationCategory Reference (second: Lifestyle). Privacy policy URL from step B4
    Age ratingNo to all mature content. Yes to user-generated content
    App PrivacySame four data types as Google: email, name, messages, other user content. Not used for tracking
    Pricing and availabilityFree, all countries you want

    App Review InformationDemo email and password. Note: "Arabic-only, right-to-left. Reminders are local notifications."

    Screenshot sizes: Apple's screenshot specifications.

  9. Submit for review

    You

    Why: a person at Apple checks the app against the review guidelines.

    • On the version page, under Build, choose the TestFlight build.
    • Pick "Manually release" if you want to choose the launch day.
    • Press Add for Review then Submit. Review usually takes 1–2 days.
    • If rejected, Apple explains why in Resolution Center. Fix, rebuild, resubmit.

F

Releasing an update later

  1. Raise the version number

    Developer

    Why: both stores reject a file with a number they have already seen.

    • In pubspec.yaml: version: 1.0.0+1 becomes 1.0.1+2. Both parts go up.

  2. Build and upload both apps

    Developer
    • Android: build the .aab (step B2) → Production → Create new release.
    • iPhone: start the Codemagic build (step C6) → new version in App Store Connect → submit.

    Updates skip the 14-day test and are usually reviewed within 1–2 days.
